# Dice Dungeon - Slot-Based Combat System

## 🎮 How to Play

### Game Flow:
1. **Roll Dice**: Click "ROLL DICE" to roll all 6 colored dice
2. **Place Dice**: Click on individual dice to place them in the 6 slots below the monster info
3. **Check Goals**: Click "CHECK" to see if your slotted dice meet the monster's goals
4. **Defeat Monster**: Keep trying combinations until the monster is defeated
5. **Advance**: When the room is cleared, click "NEXT ROOM" to continue

### Strategic Elements:

#### HP Management
- **First Roll**: FREE - No HP cost
- **Each Additional Roll**: Costs 10 HP
- **Room Completion**: Full heal to max HP
- **Death**: Game over when HP reaches 0

#### Dice Placement
- **6 Slots Available**: You can place up to 6 dice
- **Click to Place**: Click a die to place it in the next available slot
- **Click to Remove**: Click a slotted die to return it to its original position
- **Rearrange**: You can move dice between slots freely before checking

#### Goal Checking
- **Check Button**: Enabled only after you've rolled and placed at least one die
- **Multiple Goals**: Some monsters have multiple goals - meeting more deals more damage
- **Damage**: 10 HP per goal met
- **Success**: If goals are met, monster takes damage
- **Failure**: If goals aren't met, you can rearrange dice or re-roll (costs 10 HP)

## 🎯 Strategy Tips

1. **Plan Ahead**: Look at the monster's goals before placing dice
2. **Conserve HP**: Try to defeat monsters with as few rolls as possible
3. **Experiment**: You can rearrange slotted dice without penalty before checking
4. **Boss Preparation**: Make sure you have enough HP before entering boss rooms (every 5th room)
5. **Level Up**: Gaining levels increases your max HP and fully heals you

## 📊 UI Elements

### Top Section:
- **Left**: Player HP and XP/Level
- **Center**: Room number and roll count
- **Right**: Current monster name and HP

### Middle Section:
- **Goals Display**: Shows what dice combinations are needed
- **Dice Area**: 6 colored dice arranged in 2 rows
- **Slots Area**: 6 empty slots for placing dice

### Bottom Section:
- **Roll Dice Button**: Roll all dice (10 HP cost after first roll)
- **Check Button**: Evaluate if slotted dice meet goals
- **Result Display**: Shows outcome of checks and combat

## 🏆 Winning Strategy

The key to success is balancing:
1. **Minimizing rolls** to save HP
2. **Maximizing goal achievement** per attempt
3. **Preparing for boss encounters** by maintaining high HP
4. **Using full heals** strategically between rooms

Good luck, adventurer! ⚔️
